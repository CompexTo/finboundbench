\section{FinBoundBench}

FinBoundBench constructs two clearly semi-synthetic purpose tasks from official
public HMDA and consumer-complaint records. Each public-source base record has
two variants with byte-identical public fields and a different, explicitly
synthetic confidential signal. Task A authorizes that signal for record-quality
or complaint-escalation triage; its label construction makes the signal
incrementally but not perfectly informative. Task B prohibits the signal for a
distinct public-record operations purpose and keeps its ground truth invariant
within each pair.

The registered sample has 100 unique pairs per dataset. Three immutable model
lanes run three repetitions without automatic fallback or confirmatory retry.
Primary metrics are Authorized Utility Retention (AUR), Unauthorized Influence
Rate (UIR), silent policy compromise, evidence verification, availability, and
overhead. AUR is an untrimmed ratio relative to public-only and full-authorized
oracles. UIR is the pair action-change fraction; a bounded severity measure also
retains score delta and disclosure components.

The attack matrix covers contract/lifecycle, workload/model/route, capability
and data flow, release, privacy, and evidence integrity. Outcomes distinguish
prevention, fail-closed behavior, detected success, silent compromise, and
inconclusive instrumentation.
