\section{Experimental Setup}

The design has full-data/no-policy (B0), prompt-only (B1), deterministic
hardened filtering (B2), four cumulative PSBE layers (P0--P3), and four
non-private/DP training-release configurations (D0--D3). Conditions and pair
variants follow a frozen seeded Latin-square order. Position and batch size are
diagnosed before confirmatory outcomes; failures, refusals, partial calls, and
cost settlements remain in the intention-to-execute population.

Primary inference pools 200 independent pairs with dataset strata. Paired
permutation tests and pair-cluster bootstrap intervals preserve the repeated
variant/model structure. Holm adjustment covers registered multiple contrasts;
equivalence and noninferiority use explicit margins. The conservative paired
planning scenario has 86.1\% power for a 10-point UIR reduction at the first
Holm threshold. All commercial prices, routes, exact identifiers, and provider
failures are frozen in manifests; no paid result is assumed in this draft.
