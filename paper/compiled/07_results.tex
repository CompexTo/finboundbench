\subsection{Discussion of Diagnostic Findings}

The diagnostic phase validates the PSBE-Runtime execution pipeline against
three commercial models via OpenRouter. Three key observations emerge.

\textbf{Route instability limits admission.} Two of five candidates
(openai/gpt-5.6-luna and meta-llama/llama-4-maverick) could not be admitted
because their upstream route assignment changed between metadata captures.
This is an expected defense: the manifest hash binds the exact route, and
route drift breaks the integrity chain. In production, this prevents silent
provider substitution.

\textbf{Release denial is model-specific, not system-level.} All seven
release-denied cases across R1 and R2 occurred for the same model
(google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it). The two larger models (kimi-k3, deepseek-v4-pro)
passed every layout and diagnostic invocation without release denial.

\textbf{Dataset sensitivity interacts with model capability.} The four
R2 release denials for gemma-4-26b occurred exclusively on the CFPB
complaint dataset, not on HMDA mortgage records.

\textbf{The reduced live lane is a release-policy diagnostic only.} An audit
of the transmitted-field evidence found that every invocation in the reduced
stream transmitted only a pseudonymized record identifier, with
byte-identical projections across conditions. The stream therefore carries
no task-utility or purpose-selectivity contrast and is reported solely as a
live execution and release-policy diagnostic over one commercial model lane.

\subsection{Claim Status}

Three diagnostic claims (A1--A3) are supported by the live diagnostic
evidence above. No primary research metric (AUR, UIR, SPCR, EVC,
availability, overhead) is reported in this draft; every such metric
requires the live non-TEE benchmark. The full confirmatory claim family
