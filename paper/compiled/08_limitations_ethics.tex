\section{Limitations and Ethics}

The study uses public records and synthetic confidential attributes; it does
not model real internal customer facts or authorize individual financial
decisions. Semi-synthetic construction improves causal control but limits
ecological validity. Historical public outcomes may reflect institutional and
social processes and are not treated as normative targets. Protected-group
fairness and regulatory compliance are outside the claims.

The non-TEE system trusts its host and cannot prevent a malicious administrator
from reading memory, altering execution below the measured boundary, or denying
service. Paired tests detect registered forms of influence, not every semantic
or covert channel. Model/provider versions, route behavior, batching, and
stochasticity constrain external validity. DP accountant values do not alone
establish empirical privacy, and attack measurements do not exhaust possible
adversaries. Negative, failed, and inconclusive results are retained.
