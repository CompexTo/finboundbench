Two audit findings restrict the interpretation of this stream. First, an
audit of the transmitted-field evidence showed that every invocation
transmitted only a single pseudonymized record identifier; no public
financial fields and no confidential fields reached the model, so the stream
contains no task-utility or purpose-selectivity contrast. Second, the two
conditions transmitted byte-identical projections, so differences between
them reflect model nondeterminism rather than governance. Sixty-six release
denials were caused by truncated, non-JSON outputs at the 2048-token limit.
The confirmatory experiment is suspended until the transmitted-field defect
is corrected and re-validated.

\subsection{Claim Status}

Three diagnostic claims (A1--A3) describe the live pipeline diagnostics
above. No primary research metric is reported in this draft.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Claim A1 (Admission Gate):} Three of five candidate models
      passed schema, route, and cost verification. Two were excluded due to
      route instability. This validates the R0 admission mechanism.
\item \textbf{Claim A2 (Position Robustness):} Two of three admitted models
      pass all six batch layouts without release denial. One model
      (gemma-4-26b) exhibits layout-sensitive release behavior.
\item \textbf{Claim A3 (Diagnostic Execution):} 32 of 36 R2 diagnostic
      invocations passed release validation. The four release-denied cases
      are concentrated in one model on one dataset.
\end{enumerate}

The full confirmatory claim family (H1--H10) requires the live non-TEE
purpose-selective benchmark with statistical inference, and remains
preregistered and untested.

\subsection{Security and Privacy Evaluation}

Attack, differential-privacy, evidence-verification, availability, and
overhead results are intentionally withheld. Prior test-double values for
these metrics have been withdrawn: they were deterministic oracle outputs,
not experimental results. Reporting them requires live attacks against
conventional, hardened, and PSBE implementations; real DP-SGD training with
privacy accounting; real evidence bundles verified by an independent
evaluator; and measured wall-clock executions.
