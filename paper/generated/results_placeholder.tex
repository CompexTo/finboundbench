\subsection{Claim Status}

Three diagnostic claims are supported by the frozen experimental evidence.
The reduced-scope confirmatory run provides preliminary support for additional
claims. Full confirmatory hypotheses H1--H10 require the complete 200-pair
powered run with statistical inference.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Claim A1 (Admission Gate):} Three of five candidate models
      passed schema, route, and cost verification. Two were excluded due to
      route instability. This validates the R0 admission mechanism.
\item \textbf{Claim A2 (Position Robustness):} Two of three admitted models
      pass all six batch layouts without release denial. One model
      (gemma-4-26b) exhibits layout-sensitive release behavior.
\item \textbf{Claim A3 (Confirmatory Execution):} 32 of 36 confirmatory
      invocations pass all PSBE checks. The four release-denied cases are
      concentrated in one model on one dataset.
\end{enumerate}
