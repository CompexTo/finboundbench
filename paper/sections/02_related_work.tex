\section{Related Work}

Purpose-based access and distributed usage control long predate this work, and
recent systems accounts show that translating purpose limitation into data
flows and executable checks remains difficult~\cite{purposeminimization2021,usagecontrol2018}.
PSBE does not claim purpose policy as a new primitive; it operationalizes a
specific cross-layer contract and evaluates its selective-use behavior.

Confidential ML systems protect training, inference, and distributed analytics
with trusted hardware or partitioned computation~\cite{opaque2017,chiron2018,slalom2019}.
Attestation architectures and software-supply-chain systems also define mature
evidence and provenance mechanisms~\cite{rats2023,intoto2019}. Our non-TEE
study keeps the host trusted and does not treat evidence generation as novel.

AgentDojo and related agent-security benchmarks already measure utility and
security, while CaMeL moves capability/data-flow control outside model
reasoning~\cite{agentdojo2024,camel2025}. Thus the gap is narrower: paired
measurement of approved confidential utility and prohibited cross-purpose
influence, plus cross-layer compromise and evidence reconstruction, in a
financial-AI protocol with a fair hardened-filter baseline. DP-SGD and privacy
attacks are established techniques used as evaluation components rather than
claimed contributions~\cite{dp_sgd2016,membership2017}.
